What affects automatic door installation & repair costs in Hong Kong?

One of the first questions every shop owner or facility manager asks is 'how much will this cost?'. In Hong Kong, two automatic doors can look similar yet carry very different budgets. Understanding these six cost drivers helps you compare quotes clearly and avoid surprise add-ons.

Searching online for automatic door prices in Hong Kong often turns up wide, unhelpful ranges because every entrance has distinct structural and management requirements. Knowing the six key factors below lets you evaluate any repair, upgrade, or new installation quote with confidence.

1. Scope of work: repair, operator upgrade, or full new installation

Scope is the single biggest driver of cost. Targeted repairs such as replacing activation sensors, worn rollers, or a drive belt sit at the lowest tier. Upgrading only the overhead operator and track while retaining your intact glass doors and metal frame sits cleanly in the middle.

A complete new shopfront installation — including custom tempered glass leaves, stainless-steel or aluminium framing, header cladding, operator, and floor guides — involves fabrication, glass transport, and finishing works.

  • Targeted repair: sensors, rollers, belts, switches, or alignment tuning.
  • Operator upgrade: retain sound glass doors and frame, replace overhead drive kit.
  • Full new installation: framing, tempered glass, operator, sensors, and finishing.
Letting us know whether existing glass doors can be retained immediately narrows the budget range.

2. Opening width, height, and door configuration

Wider and taller glass panels are heavier, requiring higher-capacity commercial operators, heavy-duty hangers, and longer tracks. Standard single or bi-parting sliding doors offer the best cost efficiency, while narrow openings needing telescopic doors or accessible swing-door operators use specialised mechanisms.

If you do not have exact measurements yet, a straight-on photo showing floor tiles or a person nearby gives our engineers enough scale for a useful initial assessment.

  • Standard single or bi-parting sliding doors are the most economical commercial option.
  • Tall lobby glass doors require higher-load operator ratings.
  • Telescopic sliding and automatic swing doors use specialised drive hardware.
No architectural drawings are required to start — a clear entrance photo is enough.

3. Operator brand and hardware tier

Hong Kong commercial sites commonly specify Panasonic, Dormakaba (Dorma), GEZE, Nabco, or mainstream commercial drive kits and floor springs. European heavy-duty systems, Japanese mainstream operators, and value-focused commercial setups each occupy different price tiers.

Because Hwa Lai Engineering supports all popular mainstream brands, we can present clear side-by-side options matched to your traffic volume and budget rather than pushing one expensive model.

  • High-footfall lobbies & malls: choose well-margined Japanese or European commercial systems.
  • Offices & neighbourhood shops: compare mainstream Japanese and value commercial kits.
  • Transparent quotes always state the operator brand and included accessories clearly.
Always check that a quote lists the exact brand, sensor setup, and labour scope.

4. Safety curtains, access control, and power readiness

A standard setup covers activation sensors and core safety protection. Adding threshold anti-trap curtains, touchless wave switches, card/keypad/facial access integration, electric locks, backup battery units, or fire-alarm interlocking adds hardware and commissioning time.

Having a dedicated 220V power spur already available above the header also keeps electrical work straightforward. Confirming these items upfront prevents mid-project variations.

  • Standard kit: inner/outer activation sensors and safety beam protection.
  • Common add-ons: card access, touchless exit switch, remote lock, backup power.
  • Check whether a power supply point already exists above the doorway.
Confirming access control and lock preferences upfront prevents unexpected variation charges.

5. Hong Kong working hours, mall rules, and site access

Many Hong Kong shopping malls, Grade-A office towers, and residential estates restrict noisy or entrance-blocking works to after-hours night slots (e.g. after 10 pm) or weekends, and may require contractor insurance submissions or protective hoarding.

Walk-up buildings without lifts, remote locations, or extra-high lobby headers requiring scaffolding towers also affect manpower and logistics. Sharing your building type early ensures an accurate all-in figure.

  • Normal daytime weekday hours offer the most economical labour rates.
  • Mall night works, management submissions, and scaffold access should be noted early.
  • We serve Hong Kong Island, Kowloon, and the New Territories.
Mentioning mall or commercial building rules upfront avoids scheduling delays.
