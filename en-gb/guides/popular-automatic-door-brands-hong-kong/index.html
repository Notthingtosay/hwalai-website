Hong Kong automatic door brands: Panasonic, Dorma, GEZE & Nabco

When an automatic door fails or a shopfront is being renovated, two questions come up immediately: can you service our Panasonic, Dorma, GEZE, or Nabco door, and which brand lasts best in Hong Kong? Drawing on 15 years and over 500 Hong Kong installations, here is a clear, practical guide.

1. Mainstream automatic door brands seen across Hong Kong

Most commercial automatic sliding and swing doors in Hong Kong fall into Japanese mainstream systems, European heavy-duty systems, and cost-effective commercial operators. Japanese brands such as Panasonic and Nabco are popular in retail shops, offices, clinics, and clubhouses because of their smooth, quiet running and compact header profiles.

European brands including Dormakaba (Dorma), GEZE, Tormax, and Ditec are frequently specified for commercial building lobbies, hotels, and tall or heavy glass door leaves. Hwa Lai Engineering installs, troubleshoots, repairs, and maintains all popular mainstream brands in Hong Kong.

  • Japanese mainstream (Panasonic, Nabco): compact, quiet, widely used in shops and offices.
  • European heavy-duty (Dormakaba / Dorma, GEZE, Tormax, Ditec): strong load capacity for large commercial lobbies.
  • Mainstream commercial & accessible operators: flexible fit for clinics, back-of-house, and budget upgrades.
Long-term reliability depends equally on brand choice and accurate track levelling during installation.

2. If a popular brand operator fails, do you need a full replacement?

Many tenants worry that an older discontinued model requires tearing out the entire glass frontage. In practice, the tempered glass leaves and stainless-steel framing are usually still sound, and faults frequently trace back to worn hanger rollers, stretched drive belts, faulty activation sensors, or ageing controllers.

Targeted part replacement is often far more economical when the core drive unit remains healthy. Only when both motor and control board have reached end-of-life with recurring downtime does upgrading the overhead operator unit — while retaining the existing glass and frame — make better financial sense.

  • Sensors & safety beams can normally be replaced or upgraded independently.
  • Carriage wheels, belts, and floor guides are routine wear parts.
  • Obsolete overhead operators can often be upgraded without replacing intact glass doors.
Photos of the header casing, sensors, and a brief fault video help narrow the issue quickly.

3. Choosing the right brand and operator for a new Hong Kong installation

A common mistake is choosing purely by brand name without factoring in leaf weight, daily opening cycles, and header clearance. A street-level retail entrance in Mong Kok or Causeway Bay may cycle over a thousand times per day, demanding ample motor margin and straightforward servicing access.

For clinics, offices, and clubhouses, quiet motion, accurate anti-trap protection, touchless exit switches, and smooth card access integration are top priorities. Sharing your opening width, door type, and usage pattern lets our team recommend two to three well-matched options.

  • Measure approximate clear opening width, height, and door leaf weight.
  • Leave load margin for busy peak-hour pedestrian flow.
  • Confirm access control, push-button, backup power, and fire-interface needs early.
Tight ceiling clearance above Hong Kong shopfronts makes verifying header height essential before ordering.

4. Why workmanship matters as much as the brand badge

In our repair work, we regularly encounter well-known brand operators that developed loud grinding or unstable movement within two to three years. The root cause is often an unlevel track, misaligned door hangers, excessive floor-guide drag, or poorly angled sensors picking up street traffic.

Working with a local Hong Kong contractor who commissions travel limits properly and supports ongoing maintenance makes a far bigger difference to lifespan than brand marketing alone.

  • Level tracks dramatically reduce roller and belt wear.
  • Balanced door suspension lowers continuous stress on the motor.
  • Proper sensor tuning prevents false openings and reduces wear cycles.
Simple periodic track cleaning and roller checks are the most cost-effective way to extend operator life.

Yes. Send a wide entrance photo, a close-up of the header or sensors, and a short clip of the fault on WhatsApp. Our Hong Kong team can usually identify the system type and advise next steps quickly.

Usually not. If the existing tempered glass leaves and frame are in good condition, we can upgrade the overhead operator, track, and sensors while keeping your existing doors.
